

### Apply HITL Checkpoint

After returning the report to the requester, the requesting context will
consult `@.opencode/rules/human-in-the-loop.md`. If the current Autonomy
Level includes a stop after Refactor (the default `full-hitl` does), the
requester presents the checkpoint template and waits for explicit user
approval before the next Red phase. That is the requester's responsibility,
not yours — your job ends with the report.
