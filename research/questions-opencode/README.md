# research/questions-opencode/

Research questions that have OpenCode workflows (suffix `-oc`) as their subject.

Convention:
- RQ dirs as `<chapter>-<slug>/` (e.g. `1.1-model-quality-oc/`).
- The frontmatter `id:` is the stable identity (e.g. `RQ-model-quality-oc`).
- Workflow values carry the `-oc` suffix (`v5-single-context-oc`, `baseline-inline-tdd-v1-cc-oc`, …).
- Models: lab variant IDs for Portkey-routed OpenCode models (`opus-4-7-portkey`, `kimi-k2-6`, `minimax-m2-7`, `gemini-2-5-pro`, etc.).

Cross-harness RQs (Claude vs OpenCode) live under `../questions-cross/`.
