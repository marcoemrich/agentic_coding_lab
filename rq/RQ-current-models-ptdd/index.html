Confounds (binding on every finding below)

Three axes vary jointly with the model; the RQ README holds the full argument.

  1. Harness and workflow port. Two cells run Claude Code with exact-ptdd-v1-cc, eight run pi with exact-ptdd-v1-pi. A difference between an Anthropic cell and a pi cell is the sum of model, harness and port effect.
  2. The route has a demonstrated quality effect of its own. RQ-route-effect-pi F-1.3.6 measured one model (GPT-5.6 Sol) at constant harness, workflow, kata and prompt across the OpenAI subscription and Requesty at cognitive_max 4.0 against 8.0, and showed it is not a reasoning effect. The three subscription cells here carry that effect on top of the model effect. The commercial-against-open comparison that is free of it runs within the Requesty route: gemini-4-argon against the five open-weight cells (F-1.12.4).
  3. cost_usd is a list-price comparison value, never an invoice. Nothing is billed per token on either subscription, and the Requesty cells carry the catalogue tariff without workspace discounts. Read total_tokens first.

All ten cells run Predictive TDD, so they sit on the same side of the test-list boundary and the Skip-fed discipline columns stay comparable within this RQ. They remain incomparable to any ad-hoc-TDD RQ.

F-1.12.1 — Correctness on claim-office is dichotomous per run, not graded per cell

Of 50 ok runs, 46 score 1.00, one scores 0.933, one 0.867, and two score exactly 0.00. No run in the field lands between 0.00 and 0.867. The cell means below 0.90 are therefore not capability ceilings but failure rates: deepseek-v4-1-flash solves the kata perfectly in four of five runs, minimax-m3 in three of five plus one near-miss at 0.867.
