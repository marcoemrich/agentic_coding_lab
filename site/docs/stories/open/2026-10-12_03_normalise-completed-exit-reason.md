Status: open
Source: [site data export mapping](2026-10-11_05_site-data-export.md), 2026-10-12.

## Story

Five runs from 2026-05-12 carry the legacy exit reason `completed`, which means `ok`. Rewrite it to `ok` in their `metrics.json` so the aggregator needs no special case.
